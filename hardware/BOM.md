## Enclosure

3D printed, designed in Onshape alongside the internal layout. Source file:
[`Case v2.3mf`](Case%20v2.3mf). Separate openings for the SEN54's air inlet
and fan outlet, kept apart per Sensirion's own design guidance so outgoing
air isn't drawn straight back into the inlet.
